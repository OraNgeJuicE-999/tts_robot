Modeling Notes

  • Axis convention: rewriting to x-forward / y-left / z-up (ROS REP 103), which the original jetracer.xacro did not follow — it used x for left/right.
  • Ground clearance vs. axle height: the base's own 1 cm ground clearance is not the same as axle height. Wheels sit at z = 2.5 cm (their own radius) so they meet the ground — normal for a wheel well overlapping the chassis floor.
  • Isaac Sim / Blender scale check: before trusting map + robot together in sim, verify a known real-world reference (e.g. a 1 m cube) reads correctly in both — watch for Blender scene unit scale and USD stage metersPerUnit (often defaults to cm) disagreeing.

Open Items
